Delete your account

You can delete your 吃了吗 (Chi Le Ma) account and all its data at any time.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open the app and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm.

Without the app

Email gengjun.ye@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account” from the address you signed in with, or include the name shown on your profile. It is done within 30 days and you get a confirmation.

What is deleted

Your account and sign-in, your profile and leaderboard entry, reactions you sent or received, your AI usage records, reports you made, and — when deleting in the app — everything stored on your phone. Nothing is kept afterwards.

Contact: gengjun.ye@gmail.com
