吃了吗 · Chi Le Ma

Learn Chinese for the people you want to talk to. A daily session, a guide you name, and a sentence that is yours.

Why 吃了吗

In a Chinese family, “I love you” is rarely said out loud. It’s asked: 吃了吗? Have you eaten? It comes with a plate of cut fruit you didn’t ask for, with a phone call that’s really just about dinner. It’s the love language of Chinese parents.

Many of us who grew up abroad understand that love but can’t fully answer it. We understand our parents, but can’t tell them what’s on our mind. We nod along at family dinners, we’re unsure of ourselves at work and at school, and we can’t quite say the things that matter to the people we love most: a partner, a friend, a grandmother.

吃了吗 is for us. You learn Chinese bite by bite, a little each day, the way you’d share a meal. And you learn it for someone. Pick the person and the sentence you want to say to them, and the app carries you there, until one day you can say it for real.

So: 吃了吗? Have you eaten today?

— Gengjun
