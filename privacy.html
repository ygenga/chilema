Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

吃了吗 (Chi Le Ma) is a Chinese-learning app made by one person. Most of what you do stays on your phone. This page lists everything that leaves it.

Who is responsible

Gengjun Ye, [postal address — to be added], gengjun.ye@gmail.com. See the Impressum.

What stays on your phone

Your lessons, review history, words, notes, sentences you write, handwriting practice and voice recordings are stored only on your device. Voice recordings are never uploaded by the app; if you choose “Send it” on your sentence, your phone’s share sheet sends that one recording to whoever you pick.

There are no ads, no advertising IDs, no tracking across apps, and no data is sold.

AI

The companion chat, the story writer and the grading of your sentences and handwriting are produced by AI (Claude, by Anthropic). AI answers can be wrong. Anthropic processes this content to answer you, does not use it to train its models, and deletes it after a short retention period (see Anthropic's privacy center). Do not put sensitive personal information into the chat.

Processors and transfers

Supabase (database and sign-in, EU region), Anthropic (AI), Google and Apple (sign-in, if you use them), Expo (app updates), jsDelivr (stroke-order data). Each processes data under a data processing agreement. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. Daily AI usage counts are kept with your account and deleted with it. Reports are deleted with the reporting account.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Delete everything in the app under Profile → Delete account, or see how to delete your account. For anything else, email gengjun.ye@gmail.com. You may also complain to a data protection authority.

Age

The app is meant for people aged 16 and over.

Permissions

Microphone: to hear your pronunciation — recordings stay on your phone. Camera and photos: to grade handwriting you photograph. Notifications: one daily reminder at the time you choose, generated on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the app will tell you about significant changes.
